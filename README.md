# 🌐 Website Traffic Analysis Dashboard (April – May)

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Website Traffic Analysis Dashboard** designed to monitor website performance, user engagement, traffic acquisition channels, and channel effectiveness during the **April–May** reporting period.

The dashboard provides stakeholders with a quick, data-driven view of how users interact with the website, which traffic sources generate the highest volume, and which channels deliver the most engaged visitors.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Website owners and marketing teams often struggle to:

- Understand where website traffic originates.
- Identify the most valuable traffic acquisition channels.
- Measure visitor engagement quality.
- Monitor daily performance trends.
- Optimize marketing spend based on channel effectiveness.

Without a centralized analytics solution, decision-making becomes reactive rather than data-driven.

---

## 💡 Solution

Developed an interactive dashboard that:

- Tracks website traffic and engagement metrics.
- Compares performance across acquisition channels.
- Monitors daily traffic trends.
- Evaluates channel effectiveness using engagement metrics.
- Highlights top-performing traffic sources.
- Enables quick identification of optimization opportunities.

# 📈 Dashboard Sections

## 1. Session Analysis

Analyzes total sessions generated by each traffic channel.

### Sessions by Channel

| Channel | Sessions |
|----------|-----------|
| Organic Social | **61K** |
| Direct | **37K** |
| Organic Search | **33K** |
| Referral | **31K** |
| Unassigned | **1K** |
| Email | ~0K |
| Organic Video | ~0K |

### Key Findings

- **Organic Social** generated the highest traffic volume with **61K sessions**.
- **Direct traffic** contributed **37K sessions**, indicating strong brand awareness and returning visitors.
- **Organic Search** and **Referral traffic** were significant acquisition channels.
- **Email** and **Organic Video** generated negligible traffic, suggesting underutilized marketing opportunities.

---

## 2. Performance Trend Analysis

Tracks daily performance patterns for:

- Sessions
- Engaged Sessions

### Key Findings

#### Sessions Trend

- Daily sessions fluctuated between approximately **4.5K and 7.7K**.
- Peak traffic occurred around the middle of the reporting period.
- Consistent traffic levels indicate stable user acquisition.

#### Engaged Sessions Trend

- Engaged sessions ranged from approximately **2.4K to 4.2K** daily.
- Highest engagement coincided with traffic spikes, suggesting quality traffic during peak periods.
- Temporary dips may indicate campaign inactivity or lower-performing content periods.

---

## 3. Engagement Analysis

Measures engagement rate across traffic channels.

### Engagement Rate by Channel

| Channel | Engagement Rate |
|-----------|----------------|
| Organic Video | **77.30%** |
| Referral | **66.64%** |
| Organic Search | **58.21%** |
| Organic Social | **53.93%** |
| Direct | **46.35%** |
| Email | **33.33%** |
| Unassigned | **0.72%** |

### Key Findings

- **Organic Video** generated the highest quality traffic with a **77.30% engagement rate**.
- **Referral traffic** delivered highly engaged users at **66.64%**.
- **Organic Search** maintained strong engagement while contributing substantial traffic volume.
- **Direct traffic** generated high volume but lower engagement compared to Organic Search and Referral.
- **Unassigned traffic** showed extremely poor engagement, indicating potential tracking or attribution issues.

---

## 4. Channel Efficiency Analysis

Evaluates channel performance using:

- Engagement Rate
- Events per Session
- Engaged Sessions

### Key Findings

- **Referral traffic** achieved the strongest overall efficiency, combining high engagement with high events per session.
- **Organic Search** delivered a strong balance of traffic volume and engagement quality.
- **Organic Social** drove the largest traffic volume while maintaining healthy engagement.
- **Email** and **Unassigned channels** contributed minimal value.

---

## 🔍 Key Business Insights

### 1. Organic Social is the Largest Traffic Driver
Organic Social generated the highest number of sessions (**61K**), making it the primary acquisition source.

### 2. Organic Video Produces the Most Engaged Users
Although traffic volume was low, Organic Video achieved the highest engagement rate (**77.30%**), indicating exceptionally high-quality visitors.

### 3. Referral Traffic Delivers High-Value Visitors
Referral traffic demonstrated both strong engagement (**66.64%**) and high channel efficiency.

### 4. Organic Search Provides Balanced Performance
Organic Search combines substantial traffic volume (**33K sessions**) with strong engagement (**58.21%**), making it one of the most reliable acquisition channels.

### 5. Email Marketing Underperforms
Email generated negligible traffic and relatively low engagement, suggesting campaign optimization opportunities.

### 6. Attribution Quality Requires Improvement
The Unassigned channel's very low engagement rate (**0.72%**) may indicate tracking or tagging issues.

# 📋 Recommendations

### Increase Investment in Organic Social

- Continue producing high-performing social content.
- Replicate successful campaigns.
- Expand reach across additional social platforms.

### Scale Organic Video Strategy

- Increase video content production.
- Repurpose high-performing social content into video formats.
- Expand distribution through video-focused channels.

### Strengthen Referral Partnerships

- Identify top referral sources.
- Build strategic partnerships with high-performing referrers.
- Increase backlink and guest-content initiatives.

### Optimize SEO Efforts

- Improve content targeting high-intent keywords.
- Strengthen technical SEO.
- Focus on pages generating the highest engagement.

### Improve Email Marketing

- Review audience segmentation.
- Test subject lines and content personalization.
- Optimize send timing and campaign frequency.

### Fix Tracking Issues

- Investigate the Unassigned traffic source.
- Review UTM tagging standards.
- Validate analytics implementation.
